# HR grade definitions

## HR0 — Healthy

Normal healthy green leaf tissue within an infiltration spot on a tobacco leaf, showing no yellowing, no pale discoloration, and no brown necrosis, matching untreated healthy green leaf surface.

## HR1 — Very weak HR

Faint pale-green or extremely light yellowish discolored tissue within an infiltration spot, showing subtle early HR symptoms, strictly excluding distinct bright yellow chlorosis and brown necrosis.

## HR2 — Chlorosis

Distinct bright yellowed leaf tissue within or around an infiltration spot on a tobacco leaf, representing moderate chlorosis, excluding dry tan or brown necrosis.

## HR3 — Chlorosis and necrosis

A hybrid lesion spot within an infiltration spot containing both distinct bright yellow chlorosis and tan or brown necrotic dead tissue co-existing together in the same region.

## HR4 — Severe necrosis

Dry tan or brown dead tissue caused by severe HR; including collapsed tissue or holes where severely damaged tissue has crumbled away within an infiltration spot.
